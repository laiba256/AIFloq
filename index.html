How Transom Works

See the Call
in Motion

Answers & Qualifies Instantly

Every inbound call is picked up on the first ring — day or night — by an autonomous voice agent that sounds human and knows the listing.

  • Picks up instantly on Twilio / Telnyx telephony — no busy signal, no queue
  • Asks intent, budget, and timeline using the LPMAMA framework
  • Looks up the live listing and agency policy from the knowledge base
  • Sounds natural enough that callers stay on and keep talking

Books the Showing & Logs Everything

Once a lead is qualified, Transom finds an open slot, books the showing directly into the calendar, and writes the full record to your CRM — automatically.

  • Checks Google Calendar / Outlook for the agent's real availability
  • Confirms the showing time out loud with the caller before ending the call
  • Logs the lead, transcript, and outcome to your CRM (Follow Up Boss & more)
  • Ring to booked showing, typically in under a minute

Escalates to a Human at the Right Moment

Transom knows what it doesn't know. Complex, sensitive, or high-value conversations are handed off live to your team — with full context, never a cold transfer.

  • Live transfer to an agent when a call needs a human judgment call
  • Your team monitors every call in real time from the Front Desk dashboard
  • One-tap takeover mid-call whenever someone wants to jump in
  • Nothing falls through — every call ends qualified, booked, or handed off
