Frequently asked questions

What ad budget do I need?

It depends on your goals and market. You pay ad spend directly to the platform, and our management fee is separate. We recommend a starting budget on the discovery call.

Do you guarantee results?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee ROAS. We commit to a clear testing process, sound tracking and transparent reporting.

How long before I see results?

The first weeks are for testing and learning. We share what we find as it happens.

Who owns the ad account?

You do. We work inside your account, so you keep all data and access.

Do you also run Google Ads?

Yes, Google Ads is available. Tell us on the call whether it suits your business.
